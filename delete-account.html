Delete your Donkey account

For the game Donkey by MA Games.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Donkey and tap your profile.
  2. Open the Settings tab.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you can't open the game, email mohamedaslam3205@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, or include your Donkey username. We delete the account within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your sign-in, username, profile, coins, gems, items, pass progress, friends and club membership. Anonymous match and crash records may be kept for up to 90 days. Purchases cannot be refunded once the account is deleted.
