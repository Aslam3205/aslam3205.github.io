Privacy Policy

For the game Donkey & Rummy: Card Games (com.magames.donkey) by MA Games. Last updated 23 November 2026.

What we collect

  • Account: if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID, name and email address (through Firebase Authentication). Guest play uses an anonymous ID.
  • Game profile: your username, chosen avatar and cosmetics, level, coins, gems, pass progress, match results, leaderboard, tournament and Club Race results, friends and club membership.
  • Chat: messages and reactions you send in matches and clubs, so other players can see them. Names and messages pass through a word filter.
  • Purchases: if you buy gems, Google Play handles the payment; we receive only the order details needed to give you the gems. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Device and diagnostics: crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), anonymous usage statistics such as which screens and modes are used (Firebase Analytics), and the technical data needed to connect you to matches (Photon), such as your IP address and device type.
  • Advertising: rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID. Ads play only when you choose to watch one.

How we use it

To run the game: sign you in, save your progress, match you with other players, show leaderboards, give rewards, stop cheating, fix crashes and show the ads you choose to watch. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the game: Google (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play) and Exit Games (Photon). Each handles data under its own privacy policy: Google, Photon.

Your choices

  • Players in Europe, the UK and other regions where the law requires it are asked for ad consent when the game starts and can change it any time in Profile > Settings > Privacy choices.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time - see Delete your account.

Keeping your data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, progress and username are deleted; anonymous match and crash records may remain for up to 90 days.

Children

Donkey & Rummy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
