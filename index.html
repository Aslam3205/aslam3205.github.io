Donkey & Rummy

Two classic Indian card games in one app. Donkey with up to six players at the table: follow the suit, break the round when you can't, and get rid of every card - the last one holding cards is the Donkey. Rummy: 13-card Indian Rummy with jokers - make your pure sequence and declare first to win the pot.

Ranked stake tables, clubs and the weekly Club Race with 5 quick card games, weekend tournaments and the D&R Pass.
